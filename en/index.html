What Mult does

From the first line of code
to operation.

One team follows each system from the first sketch to everyday use. No handover between vendors and no orphan software after launch.

  • 01

    Development

    Web systems and apps: customer portals, management dashboards, PDF reports, APIs and integration with data providers.

  • 02

    Launch

    The system goes live with its own production environment, domain and e-mail, and with follow-up of the first users.

  • 03

    Maintenance

    Continuous fixes and improvements. Every change goes through review and automated tests before it is released.

  • 04

    Operation

    Infrastructure, monitoring and support for the users of the systems in production.

How we work

Three rules that do not change.

  • One team from start to finish. Those who build also maintain and operate. A production issue goes back to the people who know the code.
  • Changes reviewed before release. No change goes live without review and without passing the automated tests.
  • Personal data handled responsibly. Processing in line with the Brazilian data protection law (LGPD), with a privacy policy published for each product.
