01 What we do

Those who write the code look after it.

One team follows each system from the first sketch to everyday use. No handover between vendors, no orphan software after launch.

  • 01

    Development

    Web systems and mobile apps: customer portals, management dashboards, PDF reports, APIs for integrators and integration with data providers.

  • 02

    Launch

    We take the system live: production environment, domain, transactional e-mail and follow-up with the first users.

  • 03

    Maintenance

    Continuous fixes and improvements. Every change goes through review and automated tests before it is released.

  • 04

    Operation

    Infrastructure, monitoring and support for the users of the systems in production.

02 Apps

BUSKAR

  • Inventory, sales, contacts and schedule for dealers

03 How we work

Three rules that do not change.

They apply to every system Mult takes live, from the first day of development to supporting the people who use it.

A

One team from start to finish

Those who build also maintain and operate. A production issue goes back to the people who know the code.

B

Changes reviewed before release

No change goes live without review and without passing the automated tests.

C

Personal data handled responsibly

Processing in line with the Brazilian data protection law (LGPD), with a privacy policy published for each product.
