Privacy policy

Effective from 13 October 2026

1. Who is responsible for this website

This website belongs to MULT GESTAO E SISTEMAS LTDA, CNPJ (Brazilian tax ID) 68.916.372/0001-04, headquartered in Uberlândia, Minas Gerais, Brazil. Mult is the controller of the personal data processed here.

2. What this website collects

This is an informational website. It has no sign-up, no forms, no advertising cookies and no audience analytics. The only data processed are:

  • Technical access logs. IP address, date, time and browser, kept by the hosting provider so the website works and stays secure.
  • Web fonts. The typefaces are delivered by Google Fonts. To do that, your browser shares your IP address with Google.
  • Messages you send us. If you write to our e-mail address, we process your name, your e-mail address and the content of the message in order to reply.

3. Why we use it

To keep the website online and protected against misuse, to reply to people who contact us and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and we do not use data from this website for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

With the providers that host the website and the e-mail service, which process data on our behalf, and with authorities when required by law.

5. How long we keep it

Access logs are kept for the period required by law. Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and to protect legal rights.

6. Your rights

The Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) gives you the right to confirm whether we process your data, to access it, correct it, request its deletion, request portability and know who we share it with. To exercise any of these rights, write to contato@multgestaoesistemas.com.br. We reply within 15 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD).

7. Products and apps

This policy covers this website only. Each product has its own policy, in Portuguese, describing the data processed when the product is used: BUSKAR and Consulta.ai.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

Privacy and data protection questions: contato@multgestaoesistemas.com.br.
