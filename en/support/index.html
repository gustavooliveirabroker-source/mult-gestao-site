How to reach us

Contacting Mult

Write to contato@multgestaoesistemas.com.br. We reply on business days, in Portuguese or English.

To speed things up, please include:

  • the product (for example, BUSKAR or Consulta.ai);
  • the e-mail address of your account in the product;
  • what happened, on which screen and, if possible, a screenshot.

Never send a password, a verification code or card details by e-mail. Mult does not ask for this information.

Support for each product

Existing customers find help inside the product itself:

  • BUSKAR: help channels on the website and in the dealer portal.
  • Consulta.ai: help channels on the website and in the dashboard.

For the other Grupo Avante brands, use the help channels on each brand's website.

Account, privacy and data deletion

Requests about personal data are explained in the Privacy policy. To delete your account or your data in a product operated by Mult, see Data deletion.
